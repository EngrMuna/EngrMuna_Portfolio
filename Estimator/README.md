🧱 Blockwork Estimator App

A lightweight web-based construction calculator that estimates block quantity, cement bags, and sand requirements for block wall construction based on user inputs.

This tool is designed to simplify early-stage material estimation for small to medium construction projects while maintaining clear engineering assumptions.

🔍 Problem Statement

Accurate material estimation is a recurring challenge on construction sites, especially during early planning stages. Manual calculations are often time-consuming and prone to errors, leading to:

Material shortages or excess

Increased project costs

Inefficient planning decisions

The Blockwork Estimator App provides a fast, repeatable, and transparent way to estimate required materials using standard construction assumptions.

⚙️ How It Works
User Inputs

Wall length (mm)

Wall height (mm)

Estimator Outputs

Estimated number of blocks

Estimated cement bags required

Estimated sand quantity

The calculator uses metric units throughout to align with standard construction measurement practices.

🧠 Engineering Assumptions

To keep the MVP simple and predictable, the following assumptions are used:

Standard block dimensions

Standard mortar thickness

Fixed cement-to-sand ratio

No allowance for openings (doors/windows) in v1

No wastage factor applied in v1

⚠️ Disclaimer:
This tool is intended for preliminary estimates only. Final material quantities should always be verified by a qualified professional based on site conditions and approved drawings.
